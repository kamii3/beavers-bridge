Why This Works Better Than Buying Shared Leads

If you have ever bought leads from Angi, HomeAdvisor, or Thumbtack, you already know the story.

Buying Shared Leads Online

  • Sold To 5 Guys: The second a lead comes in, it gets texted to you and four other contractors. It turns into a race to the bottom on price.
  • Tire Kickers & Ghosting: People fill out a form, never pick up your phone calls, or say they already hired someone 10 minutes ago.
  • Wasted Spend: You pay $80 to $150 per click or per lead whether you win the job or not.

Beaver's Bridge Referral System

  • 100% Exclusive To You: The homeowner contacts you specifically to quote their job. Nobody else gets the lead.
  • Neighbors Trust Neighbors: When someone asks their town for a good roofer or painter, and you get recommended, you show up with trust already built.
  • Your Area Is Protected: We only partner with one roofer, one remodeler, etc., per town. We never help your competitors.
